Little Bee, Big Heart

a poem for the girl who works so hard, its you my bae

scroll and follow the bee ↓
7:00 am, rise and shine

Before the sun is out of bed,
you tie your hair and clear your head.
A little yawn, a cup of tea,
and off you go, my busy bee.

🌷
12:00 pm, lunch break?

The list gets long, the hours fly,
and still you give it one more try.
Please sip some water, have a treat,
you're allowed to rest your feet.

🌻
3:00 pm, the long stretch

When the afternoon feels gray,
remember this along the way:
every little thing you do
grows a garden, bright and new.

🌸
8:00 pm, clocked out

The sun goes down, the sky turns gold,
a tired heart, a hand to hold.
You did enough, you did your part,
and I am proud with all my heart.

🌼
9:30 pm, almost dreamland

So here's my wish when day is through:
may sleep be soft and kind to you.
You work so hard and still you're sweet,
you make my whole world feel complete.

🪻
10:00 pm, the jar is full

You're doing so well,
even when it doesn't feel like it.

with all my love 💛
